QuietFlip Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

QuietFlip is an ad-free flip clock, countdown timer and stopwatch. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who receives it and how you can delete it. It applies to the QuietFlip apps for Android, iOS, macOS, Windows and the web app at quietflip.web.app.

The short version

  • No ads, no ad networks, no tracking across other apps or websites, and your data is never sold.
  • The clock, timer and stopwatch work without an account. An account is optional and only used to sync your settings.
  • The app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports so we can improve it.
  • You can delete your account and your synced data from inside the app.

What we collect

DataWhyWhen
Account details: email address, sign-in provider (email, Google or Apple) and a unique account ID. To sign you in and sync your settings. Only if you create an account or sign in.
Synced settings: your clock preferences, such as theme, skin, custom skin names and colours, sounds, timer presets and display options. Brightness, rotation, notification choice and a running timer stay on your device. To restore the same look and behaviour on your other devices. Only if you are signed in and Sync is on.
Usage statistics: which features you use, such as starting or pausing a timer (with its length), changing a setting, picking a skin, and which screens you open. We do not record your email, account ID, or the names and colours of your custom skins in these statistics. To understand which features matter and fix what does not work. While you use the app (mobile, desktop and web where supported).
Crash reports and diagnostics: app version, device model, operating system version and the technical trace of what failed. To find and fix crashes. When the app crashes (not available on the web app).
Device and installation identifiers: a random app-installation ID, and a push-messaging token if notifications are set up. To measure usage per installation and to deliver notifications. While you use the app.

Google's Firebase services also receive standard technical information with these requests, such as your IP address, from which an approximate location (country or city) is derived, and device and browser details. We do not ask for your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

What stays on your device

Your settings, your running timer, laps and screen choices are stored locally on your device. Timer and alarm notifications are scheduled on your device and do not leave it. If you never sign in, your settings are never uploaded.

Who receives data

We use Google Firebase as our service provider. It processes data on our behalf and not for its own advertising.

  • Firebase Authentication: sign-in, including Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple if you choose them.
  • Cloud Firestore: stores your synced settings, readable only by your own account.
  • Firebase Analytics: anonymous usage statistics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: notification delivery.
  • Firebase Hosting: serves the web app, and may keep standard server logs.

Learn more in Google's Firebase privacy information. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or share it with anyone else except where the law requires it. Data may be processed in countries other than your own, where Google operates its infrastructure.

How long we keep it

  • Account and synced settings: until you delete your account.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports: kept by Firebase for its standard retention period, which we do not extend.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and synced data: in the app, open Settings, then Account, then Delete account. This removes your account and your settings from our cloud. Settings already on your device stay until you remove the app.
  • Stop syncing: turn off Sync in Settings > Account, or sign out.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the app or in your device settings.
  • Usage statistics and device identifiers: you can reset your device's advertising or app identifiers in your system settings, or ask us to delete data tied to your installation by emailing us.
  • Access, correct or erase your data, or object to processing: email us at the address below. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you have legal rights over your personal data, and we will respond within the time the law allows.

Children

QuietFlip is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you think a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and your synced settings are protected by access rules that let only your own account read or write them. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.
