Sound & alerts

Design mockup. Tap a tile to pick that sound and hear it: ticks play three times, one second apart, the way the clock ticks; alarms play two loops. Every wave moves the way its sound does.

The in-app alert always plays, even with notifications off. Classic and Chime are today's sounds; the other eight are synthesised for Quietflip, so they carry no licence.
