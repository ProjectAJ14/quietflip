Flip card craft

The new flip card, drawn from the brief's numbers. Hinges sit in notches cut into the card, the split line is a crack with a lit lip, the card is lit from above, and the flap falls and bounces once.

The clock, proposed

  • Below 80 px the notches and pins drop out; only the crack and lip stay. Drag the slider under 80 to see what the skin tiles get.
  • Paper shows why shades come from the card colour: the crack and notches stay dark on a light skin.

Today and proposed, same size, same flip

Today (#34)

  • Pins half outside the card, in the gap
  • Split line runs through the pins
  • Flat card, digits sit low
  • Flap eases in and out, no landing

Proposed

  • Pins inside notches, flush with the edge
  • Crack stops at the notches, lit lip under it
  • Lit from above, rim on the top edge
  • Falls under gravity, bounces 3.4°

How the flap moves

Flap angle over one 360 ms flip. Today's curve slows the flap down as it reaches edge-on, which nothing falling does. Proposed: it speeds up all the way down in 300 ms, lands, lifts 3.4° and settles in the last 60 ms.
